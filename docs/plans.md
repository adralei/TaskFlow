
# TaskFlow - Subscription Plans

## Free Plan

The Free plan supports a maximum of 5 workspace members.

Workspace admins can manage existing members and invitations.

## Pro Plan

The Pro plan supports a maximum of 20 workspace members.

## Member Limit Rules

When the member count reaches the maximum allowed by
the subscription plan, additional invitations are blocked.

Removing an existing member may free capacity for another
invitation.

Upgrading the plan may increase the available member limit.

Plan changes are not performed automatically by the
support assistant.
